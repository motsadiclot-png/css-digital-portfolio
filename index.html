My Academic Learning Reflection

Introduction

Starting my journey at Botswana Accountancy College has been an important transition in my academic life. Moving from secondary school to tertiary education introduced me to a different learning environments, where independence, personal responsibility and dedication play a much bigger role than before. As a first year student doing my Bachelor of Business and Data Analytics degree , I have had to adjust to new academic expectations, unfamiliar learning tools and the responsibility of managing my own time. Although the transition has presented several challenges it has also provided opportunities for personal growth and development.

Challenges Experienced

One of my biggest challenges has been managing my workload across four modules while maintaining a consistent study routine. Communication during our CSS group discussions was also difficult at times, making collaboration and coordination challenges. Additionally, completing the quizzes in Statistical Foundations for Data Analytics required consistent preparation and a good understanding of the concepts.

Another challenge was adapting to practical applications of technology. Using Microsoft Access and Excel for the first time in my Computer Technology practicals required me to familiarise myself with unfamiliar tools and develop new technical skills. I also encountered a personal academic challenge in Fundamental of Business Finance, where I came across accounting related concepts despite having dropped Accounting as a subject in Grade 10.

Strategies Used to Overcome Challenges

To overcome these challenges, I have realised the importance of dedication, discipline and effective time management. I have been working on balancing my social life with my academic responsibilities by prioritising my studies and setting aside dedicated study time. I have also recognised the importance of minimising distractions, particularly putting away my phone when studying, so that I can concentrate better. These changes have helped me become more aware of how I use my time and approach my academic responsibilities.

Lessons and Personal Growth

One of the most important lessons I have learned is that at university nobody pushes you to succeed except yourself. Unlike secondary school where teachers may provide more direct supervision, tertiary education requires students to take responsibility for their own progress. I have also developed a better understanding of financial responsibility through learning to budget my monthly P2,200 DTEF allowance. This has taught me to be more conscious of how I manage both my time and money.

Future Improvements

Going forward, I intend to improve how I manage my academic workload by planning my study sessions earlier and avoiding last-minute preparation. I also want to become more confident when interacting and communicating with others, particularly my group project members. Improving these areas will help me become a more organised, independent and effective student.

Overall, my first-year experience has taught me that academic success requires more than understanding what is taught in class. It also requires discipline, adaptability, responsibility and a willingness to continuously improve.

Group Work Experience Reflection

Working on a group assignment during my first semester at Botswana Accountancy College gave me an opportunity to understand the importance of teamwork, communication and collaboration. Reflecting on this experience through Tuckman's Five Stages of Group Development helped me understand the challenges our group faced and the lessons I gained from working with others.

1. Forming Stage

During the forming stage our group was established and we began getting familiar with one another. We discussed the assignment requirements and what needed to be accomplished. However, from the beginning arranging suitable meeting times was challenging because getting everyone to agree on a time and place was not easy.

2. Storming Stage

The storming stage presented some of our biggest challenges. Communication among group members was poor, and agreeing on meeting times became difficult. Even when we managed to meet discussions sometimes became unproductive resulting in wasted time. We also struggled with completing tasks early, which meant that some deadlines were approached at the last minute. These challenges affected our coordination and made completing the assignment more stressful.

3. Norming Stage

As the assignment progressed, I realised that our group needed better organisation and clearer communication. We needed to become more responsible with our individual contributions and make better use of the time available during meetings. This experience taught me that successful teamwork requires cooperation, commitment and respect for everyone's time.

4. Performing Stage

As we worked towards completing the assignment, we had to focus on bringing our contributions together and ensuring that the required work was completed. Despite the difficulties we experienced earlier, the group continued working towards the common goal of completing the presentation. I learned that completing group work requires more than individual effort; members must also coordinate their work and communicate effectively.

5. Adjourning Stage

After completing the group assignment, I reflected on the overall experience and the challenges we encountered. Although arranging meetings, communicating and managing deadlines were difficult, the experience helped me recognise areas where I need to improve when working with others. I learned the importance of being reliable, respecting other people's time and completing my responsibilities before deadlines.

Lessons Learned and Personal Development

Overall, this group experience taught me that effective teamwork depends on good communication, proper planning and commitment from every member. In future group assignments, I intend to improve how I communicate with others, participate more actively in discussions and encourage better time management. I also understand that meetings should have clear objectives so that everyone's time is used productively. These lessons will help me become a better team member both academically and professionally.
